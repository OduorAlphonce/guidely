Employee Onboarding Guide

Welcome to the company! This guide will walk you through your first week.

Before Your First Day

1. Complete your new hire paperwork in the HR portal (hr.company.com). This includes your W-4, I-9, direct deposit setup, and emergency contact information.
2. Order your equipment through the IT onboarding portal. You will receive a laptop, monitor, keyboard, mouse, and headset. Equipment is shipped to your home address and typically arrives within 3 business days.
3. Set up your workspace. Ensure you have a desk, chair, and a reliable internet connection. The home office stipend of $500 will be reimbursed after your first payroll cycle.

Day 1: Orientation

9:00 AM - Welcome meeting with your manager via Zoom. Your manager will provide an overview of the team, your role, and expectations for the first 90 days.
10:00 AM - IT setup session. Install the company VPN, Slack, email on your phone, and the password manager (LastPass). Your IT buddy will walk you through each step.
12:00 PM - Virtual team lunch. The team will order lunch for you (expense it under "Team Building"). Use this time to get to know your colleagues informally.
2:00 PM - HR benefits orientation. Review health insurance options, 401(k) enrollment, and PTO policies. Enroll in benefits within 30 days.

Day 2: Tools & Training

9:00 AM - Tool walkthrough: Jira for task tracking, Confluence for documentation, and GitHub for code repositories. You will receive access invitations to each platform by end of day.
11:00 AM - Code of Conduct training (mandatory, 45 minutes). Complete the training module and quiz in the LMS. A score of 80% or higher is required.
1:00 PM - Product overview session. Learn about the company's products, key customers, and the current roadmap. Read the product FAQ in Confluence.
3:00 PM - Set up your development environment. Follow the setup guide in the engineering wiki. Your tech lead will review your setup before you start coding.

Day 3: First Assignments

9:00 AM - Code review orientation. Learn the team's code review process: submit a PR, request at least two reviewers, address all comments before merging. Review the team's coding style guide.
11:00 AM - Shadow a customer support call. Listen in on a live support call to understand common customer issues and how the team resolves them.
1:00 PM - Pair programming session with your tech lead. Work on a small bug fix together. This is a great way to learn the codebase and the deployment pipeline.
3:00 PM - Write your first unit test. The team maintains 80% code coverage. Your first task is to add tests for an existing module.

Day 4: Independence

9:00 AM - Pick up your first small ticket from the backlog. Filter by the "good first issue" label. Assign it to yourself and move it to "In Progress."
11:00 AM - Attend the weekly team standup (15 minutes). Share what you worked on yesterday, what you plan to work on today, and any blockers.
1:00 PM - Documentation review. Read the team's architecture decision records (ADRs) in Confluence. Leave comments if anything is unclear.
3:00 PM - Deploy your first change. After your PR is approved, deploy to staging using the CI/CD pipeline. Your tech lead will review the staging deployment with you.

Day 5: Wrap-Up & Roadmap

9:00 AM - First-week check-in with your manager. Discuss what went well, what was confusing, and adjust your 30-60-90 day plan if needed.
11:00 AM - Set up your recurring meetings: weekly 1:1 with manager, team standups, sprint planning, and retro.
1:00 PM - Explore the company wiki. Bookmark important pages: engineering playbook, incident response runbook, deployment checklist.
3:00 PM - Join a company social channel (#random, #pet-pics, #book-club). Building relationships outside your immediate team is encouraged.
