How to File an Expense Report

Step-by-step guide for submitting and approving expense reports in the company system.

1. Before You Spend
Check that your expense is eligible. Eligible categories include: travel (flights, hotels, rental cars), client meals, team building events, office supplies, home office equipment (up to $500 one-time), internet ($75/month max), and cell phone ($50/month max for personal device users).
Ineligible expenses include: alcohol, personal meals, commuting costs (tolls, gas, parking for regular commute), coffee/ snacks, and gifts over $50.

Always obtain prior approval from your manager for expenses over $200. For expenses over $1,000, VP-level pre-approval is required. Retain all itemized receipts — credit card statements alone are not sufficient.

2. Submitting an Expense Report
Log into the expense portal at expenses.company.com. Click "New Report." Fill in the report name (format: "YYYY-MM-DD - Purpose"). Add each expense as a line item with:
- Date of purchase
- Category (select from the dropdown)
- Vendor name
- Amount (in USD, before tax)
- Description (e.g., "Client dinner at Trattoria Roma - discussed Q2 roadmap")
- Attach a photo or scan of the itemized receipt

Receipts under $25 do not require a receipt attachment, but a description is still required.

3. Approval Workflow
Once submitted, the report goes to your manager for approval. Managers should review and approve or reject within 5 business days. Approved reports are processed for reimbursement on the next payroll cycle (the 15th and last day of each month).

If rejected, you will receive a notification with the reason. Fix the issue and resubmit. Common rejection reasons: missing receipt, incorrect category, no prior approval for large amounts.

4. Expense Policy Violations
Submitting fraudulent expenses is a serious offense. First offense results in a written warning and repayment of the fraudulent amount. Second offense results in termination. Always be honest and accurate in your submissions.

5. International Expenses
International travel expenses should be submitted in USD using the exchange rate from the date of purchase. Use the company's approved exchange rate tool (xe.com/company). Attach both the foreign currency receipt and the USD conversion.

For travel booked through the company travel portal (travel.company.com), expenses are automatically recorded. You only need to add per-diem expenses (meals, local transport) manually.

6. Common Issues & Solutions
- "Receipt is lost": Submit a missing receipt affidavit form from the expenses portal. You can do this up to 3 times per calendar year.
- "Expense was denied": Check the rejection reason, correct the issue, and resubmit within 14 days.
- "Reimbursement is late": Check the payroll schedule first. If it's past the scheduled date, contact payroll at payroll@company.com.
- "Wrong amount reimbursed": Contact your manager to approve a correction, then submit an adjustment report.
